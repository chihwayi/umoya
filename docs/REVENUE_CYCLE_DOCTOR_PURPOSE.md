# Revenue Cycle & Billing - Doctor's Role

## Why Doctors Need This Module

The Revenue Cycle module for doctors is **NOT about billing or payments** (that's Accounts Department). Instead, it's about **Clinical Charge Capture** - ensuring that charges are properly captured for services doctors provide.

---

## 🎯 Doctor's Responsibilities in Revenue Cycle

### 1. **Clinical Charge Capture** 💰
- **Purpose**: Ensure charges are captured when you perform procedures, order tests, or provide services
- **Why It Matters**: If charges aren't captured, the hospital loses revenue for services you provided
- **What Doctors Do**:
  - Review charges for their patients
  - Verify that procedures they performed have charges
  - Add missing charges if something was missed

### 2. **Charge Review Before Discharge** 🏥
- **Purpose**: Review all charges for a patient before discharge to ensure accuracy
- **Why It Matters**: Prevents billing errors, ensures proper reimbursement
- **What Doctors Do**:
  - Review charge list for admitted patients
  - Verify charges match services provided
  - Approve or flag discrepancies

### 3. **Missed Charge Detection** 🔍
- **Purpose**: Identify if charges were missed for services provided
- **Why It Matters**: Missing charges = lost revenue for the hospital
- **What Doctors Do**:
  - Review "missed charges" alerts
  - Add charges for services that weren't captured
  - Example: You performed a procedure but no charge was created

### 4. **DRG Impact Awareness** 📊
- **Purpose**: Understand how your documentation affects DRG assignment
- **Why It Matters**: Better documentation = better DRG codes = better reimbursement
- **What Doctors Do**:
  - View DRG assignments for their patients
  - Understand how documentation affects DRG
  - Work with CDI specialists to improve documentation

## 🔄 How It Works

### Workflow Example:

1. **Doctor performs procedure** (e.g., appendectomy)
   ↓
2. **System auto-captures charge** (if charge master is set up)
   ↓
3. **Doctor reviews charges** in Revenue Cycle dashboard
   ↓
4. **Doctor verifies** charge is correct
   ↓
5. **If missing**, doctor adds charge manually
   ↓
6. **Before discharge**, doctor reviews all charges
   ↓
7. **Accounts Department** creates invoice from charges

| **Doctors** | **Accounts Department** |
|------------|------------------------|
| ✅ Review charges for accuracy | ✅ Create invoices from charges |
| ✅ Add missing charges | ✅ Process payments |
| ✅ Verify procedures are charged | ✅ Manage accounts receivable |
| ✅ Approve charges before billing | ✅ Send bills to patients/insurance |
| ✅ Understand DRG impact | ✅ Financial reporting |
| ❌ **NOT** creating invoices | ❌ **NOT** performing procedures |

## 💡 Real-World Example

**Scenario**: Doctor performs a surgical procedure

1. **During Surgery**: Doctor performs appendectomy
2. **After Surgery**: 
   - System should auto-capture charge for "Appendectomy" (CPT 44970)
   - If not captured, doctor adds it in Revenue Cycle
3. **Before Discharge**:
   - Doctor reviews all charges in Revenue Cycle
   - Verifies: Surgery charge ✓, Anesthesia charge ✓, OR time ✓
   - Approves charges
4. **After Discharge**:
   - Accounts creates invoice from approved charges
   - Accounts sends bill to patient/insurance

**If doctor doesn't review**:
- Missing charges = lost revenue
- Incorrect charges = billing errors
- Poor documentation = lower DRG = less reimbursement

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway

**Revenue Cycle for Doctors = Clinical Charge Capture & Review**

Doctors ensure charges are:
- ✅ **Captured** (nothing is missed)
- ✅ **Accurate** (charges match services)
- ✅ **Complete** (all services are charged)
- ✅ **Documented** (supports proper DRG)

**This is NOT about:**
- ❌ Creating invoices
- ❌ Processing payments
- ❌ Financial reporting
- ❌ Accounts receivable management
