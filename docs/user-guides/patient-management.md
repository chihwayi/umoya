# Patient Management Guide

## Overview
The Patient Management module allows you to register, search, and manage patient information in MediCore EHR.

## Features

### Patient Registration
1. Navigate to **Patients** → **New Patient**
2. Fill in required information:
   - Personal details (name, date of birth, gender)
   - Contact information (phone, email, address)
   - Medical aid information (if applicable)
   - Emergency contact
3. Click **Save** to register the patient

### Patient Search
- **Quick Search**: Use the search bar to find patients by name, ID, or phone number
- **Advanced Search**: Filter by:
  - Age range
  - Gender
  - Registration date
  - Medical aid provider
  - City/location

### Patient Profile
Each patient profile includes:
- **Demographics**: Personal and contact information
- **Medical History**: Past diagnoses, surgeries, procedures
- **Family History**: Family medical conditions
- **Social History**: Lifestyle factors (smoking, alcohol, occupation)
- **Documents**: ID cards, insurance cards, medical reports
- **Timeline**: Chronological view of all patient interactions

### Medical History Management
- Add past diagnoses with SNOMED CT coding
- Record surgeries and procedures
- Track hospitalizations
- Document injuries and accidents

### Family History
- Record family medical conditions
- Specify relationships (parent, sibling, etc.)
- Track hereditary conditions

### Social History
- Smoking status and history
- Alcohol consumption
- Occupation and workplace hazards
- Exercise and diet habits
- Travel history

## Best Practices
- Always verify patient identity before registration
- Keep contact information up to date
- Document medical history comprehensively
- Use SNOMED CT codes for diagnoses
- Maintain privacy and confidentiality
