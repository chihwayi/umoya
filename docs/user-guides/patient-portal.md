# Patient Portal Guide

## Overview
The Patient Portal allows patients to access their medical information, schedule appointments, and interact with their healthcare providers online.

## Patient Features

### Registration & Login
- Self-registration with email verification
- Secure login with email/password
- Password reset functionality
- Two-factor authentication (optional)

### Dashboard
- Overview of appointments
- Recent lab results
- Active prescriptions
- Health goals progress
- Notifications

### Appointments
- **View Appointments**: See upcoming and past appointments
- **Request Appointment**: Request new appointments
- **Cancel/Reschedule**: Manage existing appointments
- **Telehealth**: Join video consultations

### Medical Records
- View medical history
- Access lab results
- Download prescriptions
- View imaging results
- Export health records (PDF, FHIR, JSON)

### Vitals Submission
- Submit vital signs from home:
  - Blood pressure
  - Heart rate
  - Temperature
  - Weight/Height
  - Blood glucose
- View trends and charts
- Receive alerts for abnormal readings

### Prescriptions
- View active prescriptions
- Download PDF prescriptions
- Request refills
- Medication adherence tracking
- Photo upload for medication verification

### Questionnaires (PROs)
- Complete health questionnaires:
  - PHQ-9 (Depression screening)
  - GAD-7 (Anxiety screening)
  - PROMIS (General health)
  - Pain scales
  - Disease-specific questionnaires
- View response history
- Track trends over time

### Health Goals
- Set health goals (weight loss, BP control, etc.)
- Track progress
- Earn achievements
- Maintain streaks
- View leaderboards

### Telehealth
- Join video consultations
- Screen sharing
- Chat with providers
- Post-consultation summaries

### Chronic Disease Management
- Diabetes management portal
- Cardiology/Hypertension portal
- Care plan visibility
- Medication tracking
- Trend visualization

### Family Access
- Grant family members access
- View family health information
- Manage consent

### Fitness Integration
- Connect fitness apps:
  - Fitbit
  - Apple Health
  - Google Fit
  - Strava
  - Garmin
- Automatic data sync
- Activity tracking

### Symptom Checker
- AI-powered symptom assessment
- Get preliminary guidance
- Share results with doctor

## Security & Privacy
- Encrypted data transmission
- HIPAA compliance
- Consent management
- Audit trails
- Secure file storage

## Mobile Access
- Responsive design for mobile devices
- Mobile app (coming soon)
- Push notifications

## Support
Patients can contact support through:
- In-app messaging
- Email support
- Phone support
- Help center
