# Billing & Medical Aid Claims Guide

## Overview
The Billing module handles invoicing, payments, and medical aid claims processing for your clinic.

## Billing Features

### Creating Bills
1. Navigate to **Billing** → **New Bill**
2. Select patient
3. Add services/procedures:
   - Consultation fees
   - Lab tests
   - Procedures
   - Medications
4. Set payment method (cash, card, medical aid)
5. Generate invoice

### Payment Processing
- **Cash Payments**: Record cash transactions
- **Card Payments**: Process card payments
- **Bank Transfer**: Record bank transfers
- **Mobile Money**: EcoCash, OneMoney integration
- **Medical Aid**: Submit to medical aid provider

### Invoice Management
- Generate PDF invoices
- Email invoices to patients
- Print invoices
- Track payment status
- Handle refunds

### Financial Reports
- **Revenue Reports**: Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly
- **Profit & Loss**: Income vs expenses
- **Cash Flow**: Inflows and outflows
- **Aging Reports**: Outstanding payments by age

## Medical Aid Claims

### Pre-Authorization
1. Navigate to **Claims** → **Pre-Authorization**
2. Select patient and medical aid
3. Enter service details and amount
4. Submit request
5. Track approval status

### Claim Submission
- **Automatic Generation**: From bills and appointments
- **Manual Entry**: Create claims manually
- **Bulk Submission**: Submit multiple claims
- **Status Tracking**: Real-time claim status

### Claim Status
- **Pending**: Awaiting submission
- **Submitted**: Sent to medical aid
- **Approved**: Payment approved
- **Rejected**: Requires correction
- **Paid**: Payment received

### Rejection Handling
- View rejection reasons
- Correct errors
- Resubmit claims
- Track resubmission count

### API Integration
- CIMAS integration
- Premier Medical Aid integration
- Econet Health integration
- Real-time status checking

## Tax Management
- **VAT Calculation**: Automatic VAT on invoices
- **PAYE**: Employee tax calculations
- **Tax Reports**: Generate tax reports
- **Compliance**: Zimbabwe tax regulations

## Payment Reconciliation
- Import bank statements
- Match payments automatically
- Manual reconciliation
- Unmatched payment tracking

## Best Practices
- Bill immediately after service
- Verify medical aid coverage
- Submit claims promptly
- Reconcile payments regularly
- Keep tax records updated
