### 1. **Integrated Pharmacy Management System**
**Why Critical**: Clinics currently use separate pharmacy software
**MediCore Advantage**:
- Real-time prescription to dispensing workflow
- Automatic inventory management with low-stock alerts
- Drug expiry tracking and batch management
- Supplier integration for automatic reordering
- Profit margin analysis per medication
- Insurance formulary checking before prescribing

### 2. **Laboratory Information System (LIS)**
**Why Critical**: Labs use separate systems, results come via email/fax
**MediCore Advantage**:
- Direct lab result integration into patient records
- Automated critical value alerts
- Trend analysis and graphical displays
- Quality control and reference range management
- Barcode specimen tracking
- Integration with major Zimbabwe labs (Lancet, PathCare)

### 3. **Financial Management & Accounting**

- Cash flow forecasting
- Bank reconciliation automation
- Payroll integration for staff
